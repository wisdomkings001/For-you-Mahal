a letter for you

For Mahal

Something I should have said a long time ago.

Mahal,
I owe you more than sorry.

I've been sitting here — quiet, with the weight of today — not just replaying what I said, but really listening to what you told me.

You said you get anxious. Extremely anxious. Every time. And I kept dismissing it with "it's just a joke." I made your pain into something small. I turned your worry into a punchline — and that wasn't love. That was carelessness dressed up as humour.

I'm sorry, Mahal. Not just for today. For every time I did that.

✦   ✦   ✦

I hear you now.

Long distance already asks so much of us. Every silence is longer. Every distance between messages feels wider. When you talk to someone — a friend, anyone — the last thing you should have to wonder is whether your partner trusts you.

My jokes weren't just words. They were little knives. And I kept handing them to you, smiling, then wondering why you bled.

"I only get anxious, extremely anxious every time… always wondering what I did wrong only for you to say it's just a joke."

I read that line over and over. That's not you being sensitive. That's you telling me I've been failing you in a pattern — and I wasn't paying attention.

✦   ✦   ✦

What I promise you, Mahal.

No more jokes that make you feel watched, tested, or doubted. No more "it's just a joke" when your feelings are right there in front of me asking to be held.

You deserve a partner whose words build you up — especially across the distance, where words are almost all we have. I want to be that person. I'm choosing to become him — not just for you, but because of you.

You are not too much. You never were. You were just with someone who wasn't doing enough.

✦   ✦   ✦

"Don't make me hate you."

Those words broke something in me, Mahal. Because the very last thing I ever want to be is someone you hate. Someone who made love feel like anxiety. Someone you had to protect yourself from.

You don't have to say anything right now. You don't have to forgive me today. Take every second of space you need — I'm not going anywhere, and I'm not chasing. I'm just here, being still, and making sure the version of me waiting for you is worth coming back to.

I love you. Quietly. Without pressure. With both hands open.

— Always yours, always  🤍
